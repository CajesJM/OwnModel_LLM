Question: Who founded Trinidad Municipal College?
Answer: Mr. Paciano Petarco initiated the plan, with the help of Municipal Mayor Atty. Avelino N. Puracan, and it eventually became the Trinidad Junior College before being turned over to the Local Government Unit of Trinidad and renamed Trinidad Municipal College in 1997.

Question: When did TMC become a municipal college?
Answer: The college became known as Trinidad Municipal College from 1997 up to the present, after the Local Government Unit of Trinidad took over its ownership and management.
